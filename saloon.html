A little about us

A salon that keeps things personal.

Atelier Mira is a small hair and beauty studio in Indiranagar. We keep our appointments relaxed and take time to understand what you actually want.

Whether it is a regular haircut, a new colour or getting ready for a special occasion, our team focuses on making the experience comfortable from start to finish.

  • Experienced stylists
  • Personal consultation before every service
  • Quality professional products
  • Appointments and selected walk-ins
